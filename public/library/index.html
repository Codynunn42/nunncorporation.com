Executive Library

Frameworks for clearer decisions and controlled execution.

A public collection of Nunn Corporation operating principles. These summaries support orientation and assessment; they do not expose customer, government, security, or privileged operating records.

Apply the Frameworks Review Solutions

Framework 01

Outcome-First Decisions

Define the mission outcome, the decision required, the deadline, and the evidence that would justify action.

  • Outcome and success criteria
  • Decision owner and timing
  • Constraints and evidence gaps

Framework 02

Authority Before Execution

Separate recommendation, approval, execution, and proof so a prepared action is never mistaken for authorized work.

  • Named authority
  • Explicit decision gates
  • Traceable receipts

Framework 03

Evidence-Backed Readiness

Classify what is verified, what is planned, what is historical, and what remains unsupported before making claims.

  • Current verification
  • Source and timestamp
  • Open questions and holds

Framework 04

Operating Cadence

Turn strategy into a daily, weekly, and monthly rhythm with owners, deadlines, escalation paths, and closeout evidence.

  • Daily support and decision review
  • Weekly gate and closeout
  • Monthly posture review
